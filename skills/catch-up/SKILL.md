---
name: catch-up
description: Catch the user up on progress and pending decisions when they ask for a recap or return to a long-running task.
---

Explain what changed since the user's last message before this request.
Use plain language and keep the recap short.

Check relevant current state before reporting it. Distinguish completed,
running, blocked, and unverified work.

List every decision still waiting on the user, including earlier unresolved
ones. For each, give enough context to decide: what changes, your
recommendation, supporting evidence, and material risks or tradeoffs.
Link relevant artifacts, but make the reply understandable on its own.

Work you can do yourself is not a user action item. Do not invent decisions
or hide pending items to keep the reply short.

Keep this catch-up read-only. If nothing changed and nothing needs the
user's attention, say so and stop.
